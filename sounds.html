For grown-ups

Built for a dyslexic learner using structured-literacy (Orton-Gillingham) ideas: one sound at a time, in a blendable order — stretchy sounds and vowels first, harder stop sounds (b, d, t, p, k) later. Each sound is multisensory: see the letter, trace it, hear it, say it.

Say sounds cleanly, with NO extra "uh" — a crisp /b/, not "buh." That matters most for him: "buh-a-tuh" can't be blended, but a clean /b/-/a/-/t/ can. The iPad voice adds that "uh," so use the Word button as the anchor and model the clean sound yourself. The mouth cue helps him feel each sound.

Go slowly, review old sounds before new ones, keep sessions short (10–15 min), and overlearn. An app supports practice — a trained reading specialist is what changes outcomes.
