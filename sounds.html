For grown-ups

This follows how US classrooms teach early reading (phonics): the child links each letter to a sound and a keyword — "A… apple… /a/". Vowels (a, e, i, o, u) are marked in amber because they behave differently and matter most for sounding out words.

Honest limit: the iPad voice can't make a perfectly crisp sound for letters like b, d, k, p, t — it adds a small "uh" ("buh" instead of a clean /b/). Use the word button as the reliable anchor, and model the clean sound yourself when you can. Picking a high-quality voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English helps.
